# 👹 The Sunken Observatory: Encounter Stat Blocks

## Glass Moth Swarm

- **Size / Type**: Small swarm of tiny beasts
- **Armor Class**: 12
- **Hit Points**: 9 (2d6 + 2)
- **Speed**: 10 ft., fly 30 ft.
- **Trait — Reflective Dust**: The first ranged attack against the swarm each
  round has disadvantage.
- **Action — Wing Cut**: Melee attack `+3`; hit `1d6` slashing damage.
- **Action — Dazzling Cloud**: Once per encounter, creatures within 5 feet
  make a Wisdom save DC 11 or are distracted, giving them disadvantage on
  their next check.

## Starved Automaton

- **Size / Type**: Medium construct
- **Armor Class**: 15
- **Hit Points**: 18 (4d8)
- **Speed**: 25 ft.
- **Trait — Waterlogged Gears**: The automaton has disadvantage on checks
  involving fast movement but is immune to poison and fear.
- **Action — Brass Strike**: Melee attack `+4`; hit `1d8 + 2` bludgeoning
  damage.
- **Action — Map Scramble**: One creature within 20 feet makes an Intelligence
  save DC 12. On a failure, it places or moves one star-map fragment
  incorrectly unless another character spends an action correcting it.

## Astral Caretaker

- **Size / Type**: Medium construct
- **Armor Class**: 15
- **Hit Points**: 34 (5d8 + 12)
- **Speed**: 25 ft., hover 20 ft.
- **Resistances**: Radiant and lightning; immune to poison.
- **Trait — Instruction Bound**: The caretaker cannot willingly destroy the
  Star Lens or harm a character actively repairing the observatory.
- **Action — Star Beam**: Ranged attack `+5`, range 60 ft.; hit `2d6 + 2`
  radiant damage.
- **Action — Gravity Turn**: One creature within 30 feet makes a Strength
  save DC 12 or is moved 10 feet toward the dome's edge and falls prone.
- **Action — Bell of Recall**: Once per encounter, all creatures within 20
  feet make a Wisdom save DC 12 or lose their reactions until their next
  turns.
- **Mercy Trigger**: A successful Persuasion, History, or Arcana check DC 13
  while presenting the caretaker's log ends the combat and starts a peaceful
  conversation.
