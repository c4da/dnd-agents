# 👹 The Wolf Host at Castra Luna: Encounter Stat Blocks

## Wolf Pack Surge

- **Size / Type**: Huge swarm of supernatural beasts
- **Armor Class**: 14
- **Hit Points**: 30 (4d12 + 4)
- **Speed**: 50 ft.
- **Pack Strength**: Each active surge counts as one Pack Strength unit. At
  half hit points, it has disadvantage on its next coordinated action.
- **Action — Many Bites**: Up to three creatures within 10 feet make a
  Dexterity save DC 13 or take `2d6` piercing damage and fall prone.
- **Action — Wall Climb**: Move across a wall or barricade without a check.
  The surge cannot attack on the same turn unless it begins on the wall.
- **Action — Break and Circle**: Withdraw 20 feet and give the next pack
  surge advantage on its first attack.

## Moon-Touched Legionary

- **Size / Type**: Medium humanoid, cursed shapeshifter
- **Armor Class**: 15 (shield and segmented armor)
- **Hit Points**: 26 (4d8 + 8)
- **Speed**: 30 ft.
- **Trait — Formation Memory**: While adjacent to an allied legionary or wolf,
  the Moon-Touched Legionary gains `+1` Armor Class.
- **Action — Gladius and Shield**: Melee attack `+6`; hit `1d6 + 4`
  slashing damage. On a hit, the target cannot take reactions until its next
  turn.
- **Action — Pilum Cast**: Ranged attack `+5`, range 20/60 ft.; hit `1d8 + 3`
  piercing damage.
- **Action — Pack Command**: One Wolf Pack Surge within 30 feet moves up to
  half its speed as a reaction.
- **Release Clue**: Calling the legionary by its human name and succeeding on
  Persuasion or Religion DC 14 prevents Pack Command for one round.

## Trench Wraith

- **Size / Type**: Medium undead spirit
- **Armor Class**: 14
- **Hit Points**: 24 (5d8 + 2)
- **Speed**: 30 ft., ignores difficult terrain
- **Trait — Unfinished Order**: The Wraith cannot willingly move farther from
  the battlefield standards.
- **Action — Standard Spear**: Melee attack `+5`; hit `1d8 + 3` necrotic
  damage.
- **Action — Repeat the Charge**: One creature within 20 feet makes a Wisdom
  save DC 13 or moves 10 feet toward the nearest enemy and uses its reaction
  to make one weapon attack if possible.

## Iron Howler

- **Size / Type**: Huge supernatural spirit
- **Armor Class**: 17
- **Hit Points**: 78 (8d12 + 26)
- **Speed**: 40 ft., climb 30 ft.
- **Resistances**: Cold, necrotic, and nonmagical weapon damage.
- **Immunities**: Frightened, charmed, and exhaustion.
- **Trait — Commanding Howl**: At the start of each round, one Wolf Pack Surge
  can move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Action — Iron Claw**: Melee attack `+8`; hit `2d8 + 5` slashing damage.
- **Action — Battlefield Roar**: Creatures within 30 feet make a Wisdom save
  DC 15 or take `2d6` psychic damage and lose their reactions until their
  next turns. A creature that hears a spoken true name has advantage.
- **Action — Standards in the Wind**: Two spectral standards appear in
  unoccupied spaces. A creature within 5 feet of one makes a Dexterity save
  DC 14 or takes `1d8` necrotic damage and is restrained until it uses an
  action to break free.
- **Phase Two — The Last Order**: At 39 hit points, the Iron Howler repeats
  the command to hold the line. Until a release action succeeds, all Wolf
  Pack Surges gain advantage on their next coordinated action.
- **Mercy Trigger**: After two successful release actions, the Iron Howler
  speaks with a dead legionary's voice and loses its resistances for one
  round. A successful final release action ends the encounter without killing
  it.
