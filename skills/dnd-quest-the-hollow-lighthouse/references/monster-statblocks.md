# 👹 The Hollow Lighthouse: Encounter Stat Blocks

## Brine Mote

- **Size / Type**: Tiny elemental
- **Armor Class**: 13
- **Hit Points**: 5 (2d4)
- **Speed**: 20 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Traits**: Can move through a space as narrow as one inch. It cannot
  willingly move more than 30 feet from standing water.
- **Action — Salt Spray**: Ranged attack `+3`, range 20 ft.; hit `1d4 + 1`
  cold damage and the target has disadvantage on its next Athletics check.

## Hollow Lighthouse Warden

- **Size / Type**: Medium bound spirit
- **Armor Class**: 14
- **Hit Points**: 30 (6d8 + 3)
- **Speed**: 0 ft., hover 20 ft.
- **Resistances**: Cold; nonmagical weapon damage while inside the lantern
  chamber.
- **Beacon Pulse**: At the end of each round, one creature within 15 feet
  makes a Constitution save DC 12 or is blinded until the end of its next
  turn.
- **Action — Glass Hook**: Melee attack `+4`; hit `1d8 + 2` slashing damage.
- **Action — Drowning Memory**: One creature within 30 feet makes a Wisdom
  save DC 12. On a failure it loses its reaction and moves 10 feet toward the
  nearest water at the start of its next turn.
- **Mercy Trigger**: At 15 hit points or fewer, the Warden can be calmed by a
  successful Persuasion, Religion, or Arcana check DC 12. A character who
  presents Maelin's journal has advantage.

## Reef Hound

- **Size / Type**: Medium beast
- **Armor Class**: 12
- **Hit Points**: 11 (2d8 + 2)
- **Speed**: 40 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Action — Bite**: Melee attack `+4`; hit `1d6 + 2` piercing damage.
- **Action — Drag**: If the bite hits a creature standing in water, the hound
  can pull it 10 feet on a failed Strength save DC 11.
