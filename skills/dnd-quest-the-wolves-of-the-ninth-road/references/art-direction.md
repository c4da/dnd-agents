# 🎨 Art Direction: The Roman Frontier & Werewolf Aesthetic

This document establishes the official visual style, lighting ratios, composition, and prompt specifications for *The Wolves of the Ninth Road* and its companion campaigns, based on the official campaign cover artwork (`assets/the-wolves-of-the-ninth-road-flyer.jpg`).

---

## 🏛️ The Visual Canon

The style fuses historical Roman frontier military realism with gothic lycanthropic survival horror. The core emotional theme is **defiant human discipline standing against an overwhelming ancient predator**.

```text
================================================================================
                    VISUAL COMPOSITION HIERARCHY
================================================================================
 [ UPPER BACKDROP ]
   - Giant cratered full moon in a deep midnight-indigo starry sky
   - Jagged mountain ridge with dying golden sunset glow on the far horizon
   - Ruined stone Roman watchtower / arches crumbling on a high mountain crag
   - Colossal bipedal werewolf silhouette perched atop the stone ledge

 [ MIDGROUND ]
   - Deep untracked snowdrifts punctuated by large lupine paw tracks
   - Fortified perimeter with sharp wooden palisade stakes and supply crates
   - Twisted dark evergreen pines clinging to frostbitten cliffs

 [ FOREGROUND BIVOUAC ]
   - Weary Roman legionaries in battered iron lorica segmentata armor
   - Montefortino / Gallic helmets with brass fittings and neck guards
   - Dirt-and-blood-stained crimson wool cloaks and tunics
   - Drawn iron gladii and curved rectangular red scutum shields
   - Crimson Roman legion vexillum standard fluttering in the icy gale
   - Crackling central campfire casting bright amber reflections on steel
================================================================================
```

## 💡 Lighting Design: The Tri-Light System

The signature atmosphere is driven by high-contrast, dual-temperature chiaroscuro:

1. **Warm Key Light (Campfire & Braziers)**:
   - Color: Deep amber (`#FFA020`) to molten gold (`#FFCC40`).
   - Function: Illuminates the soldiers' segmented metal plates, rivets, rugged weathered faces, and timber crates, providing psychological warmth and safety.
2. **Cold Ambient & Rim Light (Moonlight & Winter Snow)**:
   - Color: Silvery-white (`#E0E8FF`) to midnight indigo (`#182038`).
   - Function: Wraps around the snowfields, the cliff faces, and the menacing outline of the werewolf perched above.
3. **Horizon Twilight Gradient**:
   - Color: Burnt orange fading to dusk violet.
   - Function: Silhouettes the distant mountain peaks, accentuating the remote isolation of the frontier outpost.

## 🎬 Key Scene Presets

### Scene 1: The Outpost Twilight Watch (Cover Scene)
- **Focus**: The weary soldiers warming their hands by the fire as the werewolf watches silently from the watchtower crag.
- **Tone**: Suspense, dread, quiet vigilance before the assault.

### Scene 2: The Testudo in the Blizzard
- **Prompt**:
  `Gritty Roman legionaries locked in a tight defensive testudo shield wall, red scutum shields battered and scarred with deep claw marks, raging winter blizzard with driving horizontal snow, three massive supernatural wolves with glowing yellow eyes leaping through the whiteout, cinematic action framing, dramatic low lighting.`

### Scene 3: The Broken Shrine at Dawn (Hopeful Resolution)
- **Prompt**:
  `Dawn breaking over an ancient snowbound mountain shrine, pale golden morning rays cutting through mist, a wounded Roman legionary kneeling in the snow with a shattered wolf mask beside him, fellow soldiers wrapping him in a wool cloak with solemn relief, peaceful alpine winter sunrise, hopeful atmosphere.`
