# 👹 The Ember Vault: Encounter Stat Blocks

## Cinderling

- **Size / Type**: Small elemental
- **Armor Class**: 13
- **Hit Points**: 8 (2d6 + 1)
- **Speed**: 30 ft.
- **Trait — Kindled Body**: A creature that hits the Cinderling with a
  melee attack while within 5 feet takes `1` fire damage.
- **Action — Coal Spit**: Ranged attack `+4`, range 30 ft.; hit `1d6 + 2`
  fire damage.
- **Action — Flashstep**: Once per encounter, move up to 20 feet without
  provoking opportunity attacks.

## Ash-Iron Sentinel

- **Size / Type**: Large construct
- **Armor Class**: 16
- **Hit Points**: 38 (5d10 + 10)
- **Speed**: 25 ft.
- **Resistances**: Fire; nonmagical weapon damage while adjacent to the
  ember core.
- **Trait — Engine Protocol**: The sentinel cannot willingly move farther
  than 60 feet from the ember core. It prioritizes stabilizing the engine
  over attacking an unconscious creature.
- **Action — Hammer Arm**: Melee attack `+5`; hit `2d6 + 3` bludgeoning
  damage.
- **Action — Vent Burst**: Creatures within 10 feet make a Dexterity save
  DC 12 or take `2d6` fire damage, half on success. The sentinel cannot use
  this action in two consecutive rounds.
- **Mercy Trigger**: A successful Persuasion, Arcana, or relevant tools check
  DC 12, made while explaining the workers' survival plan, pauses the
  sentinel for one round.

## Tunnel Striker

- **Size / Type**: Medium beast
- **Armor Class**: 12
- **Hit Points**: 12 (2d8 + 3)
- **Speed**: 35 ft., climb 20 ft.
- **Action — Claw**: Melee attack `+4`; hit `1d6 + 2` slashing damage.
- **Action — Dust Cloud**: Once per encounter, creatures within 10 feet make
  a Constitution save DC 11 or are blinded until the end of their next turn.
