# 🎬 Tabletop Scene Prompt Templates & Milestone Presets

This reference contains pre-crafted scene prompts mapped directly to the official quest modules and universal campaign milestones.

---

## 🏛️ Quest Module Templates

### 1. `dnd-quest-the-lost-shrine` (The Lost Shrine of the Sun-Stone)
- **Act I Ambush**:
  `Moody forest ravine, moss-covered crumbling stone barrows, three goblins perched on wet pine logs drawing crude shortbows, misty autumn drizzle, fallen yellow leaves, tension, cinematic framing.`
- **Act III Altar Chamber**:
  `Subterranean cyclopean temple, crumbling basalt pillars, massive bugbear chieftain Kragthor clad in crude iron scrap wielding a spiked morningstar, glowing golden Sun-Stone pulsing on a carved serpent altar, floating dust motes, volumetric god rays.`

### 2. `dnd-quest-the-hollow-lighthouse`
- **Storm-Lashed Cliff**:
  `Black rocky coastal crag at midnight, crashing frothing white ocean waves, towering spiral stone lighthouse with a dark unlit iron lantern housing, jagged lightning cutting through purple tempest clouds, rain streaks, wet reflective slate.`
- **Flooded Cistern**:
  `Submerged subterranean vault beneath a lighthouse, emerald sea water rising along ancient spiral stone stairs, rusted iron dive suits, barnacle-encrusted arches, bioluminescent jellyfish drift.`

### 3. `dnd-quest-the-ember-vault`
- **Overheating Magma Core**:
  `Subterranean dwarven industrial foundry, massive cracked iron boiler vessel glowing cherry-red with internal heat, venting white superheated steam, rivers of bubbling molten slag below grated iron catwalks, heat distortion ripples, sparks flying.`

### 4. `dnd-quest-the-sunken-observatory`
- **The Submerged Star Dome**:
  `Sunken celestial observatory hall beneath crystalline blue waters, giant brass armillary sphere and astrolabe gears covered in coral growth, sunlight filtering through green glass dome overhead, schools of silver fish darting past astrological murals.`

### 5. `dnd-quest-the-red-tithe`
- **The Ossuary Cathedral & Soul Bell**:
  `Grimdark gothic subterranean ossuary, towering walls constructed entirely of bleached human skulls and interlocking rib cages, colossal black iron bell suspended by heavy rusted chains over a pit of glowing crimson embers, suffocating gothic dread.`

### 6. `dnd-quest-the-wolves-of-the-ninth-road`
- **The Snow-Choked Roman Road**:
  `Winter blizzard along an ancient Roman stone military road, frozen pine forest silhouettes, Roman legionaries holding battered red scutum shields in a tight defensive testudo, pair of massive supernatural wolves with glowing yellow eyes circling through the snowdrifts.`

### 7. `dnd-quest-the-wolf-host`
- **The Siege of Castra Luna**:
  `Midnight fortress siege in deep snow, wooden palisades reinforced with Roman turf ramparts, burning pitch braziers illuminating a vast pack of hundreds of supernatural wolves gathering along the forest ridge under a full blood moon, epic tension.`

## ⛺ Universal Tabletop Milestones

### 🔥 Campfire Haven (Long Rest)
```text
Intimate tabletop party resting around a small crackling campfire, deep in a sheltering pine grove at twilight, a knight in dented plate armor sharpening his sword with a whetstone, a bearded barbarian snoring wrapped in bear furs, an elven rogue carving a wooden trap trigger, embers floating toward star-filled sky, warm golden firelight against cool forest dusk.
```

### 🏆 Boss Defeat (The Final Blow)
```text
Dramatic climactic aftermath of a dungeon boss battle, colossal fallen monster lying motionless on cracked mosaic tiles, victorious adventuring party standing over the foe catching their breath, smoke rising from a shattered arcane staff, a paladin lowering his greatshield with reverent relief, dawn light breaking through a high fissure.
```

### 💎 The Unsealed Sarcophagus (Looting the Relic)
```text
Carved ancient royal tomb chamber, heavy stone sarcophagus lid slid ajar, glowing radiant golden light spilling out from inside onto the faces of wide-eyed adventurers, skeletal king holding a jeweled shortsword and gold coins, cobwebs, ancient dust swirls.
```

### 🍺 The Cozy Tavern (Campaign Start)
```text
Bustling medieval tavern interior, heavy oak beams, roaring stone fireplace, tallow candles on round tables, tankards of frothing dark ale, hearty bread loaves, hooded adventurers whispering over a rolled-up parchment map in a shadowy corner booth, laughter and tavern ambiance.
```
