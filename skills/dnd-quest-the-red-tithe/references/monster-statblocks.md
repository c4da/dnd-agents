# 👹 The Red Tithe: Encounter Stat Blocks

## Carrion Saint

- **Size / Type**: Medium undead
- **Armor Class**: 14
- **Hit Points**: 24 (5d8 + 2)
- **Speed**: 25 ft.
- **Resistances**: Necrotic; nonmagical weapon damage from attacks made
  against it while it is adjacent to a corpse.
- **Trait — Borrowed Face**: The Saint can imitate the voice of a dead NPC
  the party has met. A successful Insight check DC 13 identifies the imitation.
- **Action — Nail-Flail**: Melee attack `+5`; hit `1d8 + 3` bludgeoning damage.
- **Action — Funeral Breath**: Creatures within 10 feet make a Constitution
  save DC 12 or take `1d6` necrotic damage and cannot regain hit points until
  the end of their next turn.
- **Mercy Trigger**: Speaking the name of a person the Saint failed to save
  and succeeding on Religion or Persuasion DC 13 causes it to lose its next
  action.

## Gorebound Penitent

- **Size / Type**: Medium humanoid, corrupted
- **Armor Class**: 13
- **Hit Points**: 15 (3d8 + 2)
- **Speed**: 30 ft.
- **Trait — Shared Wound**: The first time the Penitent takes damage each
  round, one adjacent Penitent takes `1` necrotic damage.
- **Action — Cleaver**: Melee attack `+4`; hit `1d8 + 2` slashing damage.
- **Action — Chain Pull**: Ranged attack `+4`, range 20 ft.; hit `1d6`
  bludgeoning damage and the target moves 10 feet toward the Penitent.

## Ashen Deacon

- **Size / Type**: Large undead
- **Armor Class**: 15
- **Hit Points**: 42 (5d10 + 15)
- **Speed**: 25 ft.
- **Resistances**: Fire and necrotic.
- **Trait — Censer Smoke**: The area within 5 feet of the Deacon is lightly
  obscured. A creature that starts its turn there makes a Constitution save
  DC 12 or has disadvantage on its next attack.
- **Action — Reliquary Mace**: Melee attack `+6`; hit `2d6 + 3` bludgeoning
  damage.
- **Action — Ashen Verdict**: One creature within 30 feet makes a Wisdom save
  DC 13 or takes `2d6` psychic damage and becomes frightened until the end of
  its next turn.
- **Mercy Trigger**: If a chapel objective is completed, the Deacon withdraws
  for one round and can be questioned.

## The Red Cantor

- **Size / Type**: Large ritual intelligence
- **Armor Class**: 16
- **Hit Points**: 72 (8d10 + 28)
- **Speed**: 20 ft., hover 20 ft.
- **Resistances**: Necrotic, psychic, and nonmagical weapon damage.
- **Immunities**: Frightened and charmed.
- **Trait — Bell-Bound**: The Cantor cannot move more than 30 feet from the
  black bell. It loses its resistances while a ritual chain is severed.
- **Action — Choir Lash**: Melee attack `+7`; hit `2d8 + 4` necrotic damage.
- **Action — Name Theft**: One creature within 60 feet makes a Charisma save
  DC 14. On a failure, it cannot use its name in an action or spell until the
  end of its next turn and takes `1d8` psychic damage.
- **Action — Bellquake**: All creatures within 20 feet make a Dexterity save
  DC 14 or take `2d6` bludgeoning damage and fall prone. The Cantor cannot
  use this action in two consecutive rounds.
- **Phase Two — Choir of Teeth**: At 36 hit points, spectral mouths appear
  around the chamber. At the start of each round, one unoccupied space
  becomes hazardous; a creature entering it takes `1d6` necrotic damage.
- **Mercy Trigger**: If the party presents three rescued names and completes
  one true-name, true-sin, or true-mercy action, the Cantor must answer one
  question honestly and loses its resistances for one round.
