# 👹 The Wolves of the Ninth Road: Encounter Stat Blocks

## Moon-Wolf

- **Size / Type**: Medium beast, supernatural
- **Armor Class**: 13
- **Hit Points**: 14 (3d8)
- **Speed**: 40 ft.
- **Senses**: Keen hearing and smell; advantage on checks to track a wounded
  creature.
- **Action — Bite**: Melee attack `+4`; hit `1d8 + 2` piercing damage.
- **Action — Pack Howl**: Once per encounter, allied wolves within 30 feet
  can move up to half their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Mercy Clue**: A Moon-Wolf avoids a character carrying the silver pendant
  or speaking the name of a fallen legionary.

## Cursed Legionary

- **Size / Type**: Medium humanoid, cursed
- **Armor Class**: 14 (shield and leather)
- **Hit Points**: 22 (4d8 + 4)
- **Speed**: 30 ft.
- **Trait — Moon Shift**: While below half hit points or under direct
  moonlight, the legionary gains advantage on its next attack but cannot
  speak until the end of its next turn.
- **Action — Gladius**: Melee attack `+5`; hit `1d6 + 3` piercing damage.
- **Action — Pilum**: Ranged attack `+4`, range 20/60 ft.; hit `1d6 + 2`
  piercing damage.
- **Action — Wolf Lunge**: Move up to 20 feet and make a bite attack. On a
  hit, the target makes a Strength save DC 12 or falls prone.
- **Release Clue**: Calling the legionary by its human name and succeeding on
  Persuasion, Medicine, or Religion DC 13 ends its Moon Shift for one round.

## Winter Revenant

- **Size / Type**: Medium undead spirit
- **Armor Class**: 13
- **Hit Points**: 18 (4d8)
- **Speed**: 30 ft., ignores difficult snow terrain
- **Trait — Cold Memory**: A creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of
  the Revenant makes a Wisdom save DC 12 or loses its reaction until its next
  turn.
- **Action — Frosted Spear**: Melee or ranged attack `+4`, range 20 ft.; hit
  `1d8 + 2` cold damage.
- **Action — Whiteout**: Once per encounter, create a 10-foot cloud of snow
  for one round. Attacks through it have disadvantage.

## Wolf-Centurion Gaius Aemilius

- **Size / Type**: Large humanoid, cursed shapeshifter
- **Armor Class**: 16
- **Hit Points**: 52 (7d10 + 14)
- **Speed**: 35 ft., climb 20 ft.
- **Resistances**: Cold and nonmagical weapon damage.
- **Trait — Centurion's Command**: Once per round, one allied Moon-Wolf can
  move up to its speed as a reaction.
- **Action — Gladius of the Oath**: Melee attack `+6`; hit `2d6 + 4`
  slashing damage.
- **Action — Howling Charge**: Move up to 30 feet in a straight line. The
  first creature hit makes a Strength save DC 14 or is knocked prone and
  takes `1d8` additional damage.
- **Action — Moon Howl**: Creatures within 20 feet make a Wisdom save DC 14
  or become frightened until the end of their next turn. A creature that
  hears a spoken name of a fallen legionary has advantage.
- **Mercy Trigger**: At 26 hit points or fewer, the centurion pauses when a
  ritual action succeeds. A Persuasion or Religion check DC 14 can make the
  pause last until the next ritual action.
