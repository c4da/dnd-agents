# 🏰 Classic Computer-RPG-Style Starting Origins

When starting a campaign, the Dungeon Master presents or assigns one of these atmospheric origins based on the player's class, race, or preference:

---

## 🌾 Origin 1: The Ash-Fall of Elmridge (The Massacred Village)
*Recommended for: Fighter, Barbarian, Ranger, Paladin, or any hero with humble roots.*

### The Prologue
> *"For twenty winters, Elmridge was your world: a peaceful hamlet of thatched cottages, golden rye fields, and laughing children dancing by the millstream. Tonight was the Midsummer Harvest Feast. Bonfires crackled, fiddles played, and elderberries fermented into sweet wine.*
>
> *Then came the fog.*
>
> *At midnight, black-fletched fire arrows fell like meteors. The haylofts burst into roaring infernos. Harsh horn blasts shook the hills as raiders in jagged blackened iron swept through the village, slaughtering livestock and villagers alike. The mill wheel turned red in the burning stream.*
>
> *Captain Roderick, the grey-bearded village watchman who taught you how to hold a sword, shoves you behind the smoking stones of the well, clutching a fatal wound in his chest. With trembling hands, he presses his notched steel blade into your grasp:*
>
> *'They're not after grain, child... they came for the seal beneath the barrows... Take the survivors... run into the crags... and avenge us.'*
>
> *Behind you, the chapel bell collapses into the roaring flames. You turn to face the smoke. A raider steps through the embers, spiked club raised high. Roll for Initiative!"*

## 🏛️ Origin 2: The Besieged Academy of Neverwinter
*Recommended for: Wizard, Sorcerer, Cleric, Artificer.*

### The Prologue
> *"The grand marble halls of the Academy of High Magic have stood untouched for seven centuries. Tonight was your final trial before the Arch-Mages. As the crystal orb was passed into your hands, the protective warding runes suddenly shattered with a deafening thunderclap.*
>
> *Glass rains from the vaulted skylights. Shadow stalkers clad in dark silk slip down ropes, hurling vials of choking alchemist's fire. The Headmaster crumples with three black needles in his neck. Arcane sirens scream through the corridors. You grab your spellbook and arcane focus from the burning lectern. Two assassins turn toward you with curved blades dripping venom!"*

---

## ⚓ Origin 3: The Sluices of the Sunken Guild
*Recommended for: Rogue, Bard, Warlock.*

### The Prologue
> *"A rat's den is safer than the Thieves' Guild when the gold runs dry. You were counting your cut in the cellar beneath the fishmongers' wharf when the Guildmaster choked on his spiced wine, clutching his blackened throat. The lantern was kicked over.*
>
> *Crossbow bolts splinter the wine barrels. The rival syndicate kicks in the iron grates—they've bought off the watch and sealed the street exits. Your only path of survival is dropping through the rusted floor grate into the swirling, stinking tidal sluices that drain out into the monster-haunted marshes!"*

---

## ❄️ Origin 4: The Broken Seals of Iron Peak (Mountain Monastery)
*Recommended for: Monk, Druid, Cleric, Barbarian.*

### The Prologue
> *"At the highest peak of the Cloudspine Mountains, the monastery of the Silent Bell stands enveloped in perpetual blizzards. For three centuries, your order guarded the iron pit at the center of the cloisters.*
>
> *During evening meditation, a tremor splits the granite floor. The sacred chains snap with the sound of cannon fire. A foul, freezing stench billows from the dark abyss below as ancient slumbering horrors drag their emaciated claws over the edge. The Abbot's staff shatters—it falls to you to carry the final warning down the mountain!"*
